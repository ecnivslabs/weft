Inside .sci

ASCII picture

Each frame is a composition of colored glyphs. The decoder draws those symbols to produce the picture.

Sound and seeking

Audio, frame timing and seek points share the archive. A player can read it progressively and move through the timeline.

Your stack

Use the Rust libraries or CLI. The same decoder runs in browser workers through WebAssembly.
